> **Your AI-powered personal trainer for Special Forces level fitness** 🎖️

Bootcamper is an intelligent fitness application that provides personalized workout routines based on your daily condition, goals, and progress. Think of it as having a world-class personal trainer available 24/7, designed to help you achieve "Special Forces level" fitness through consistent, adaptive training.

## 🌟 What Makes Bootcamper Special?

### 🤖 AI-Powered Personalization
- **Smart Routine Generation**: Our AI creates customized workouts just for you, considering your daily condition, goals, and progress
- **Adaptive Training**: Your routines automatically adjust based on your mood, energy levels, and sleep quality
- **Multiple Training Styles**: Choose your perfect coach - from motivating Drill Sergeant to supportive Friend, caring Lover, or elite Elite Trainer
- **Real-time Chat**: Have conversations with your AI trainer for motivation, advice, and guidance

### 📊 Progress Tracking & Motivation
- **Military Rank System**: Progress through ranks from 훈련병 (Private) to 원수 (General) as you build consistency
- **Daily Streaks**: Track and celebrate your consecutive workout days with fun animations
- **Mood & Energy Monitoring**: Log how you're feeling each day to get better recommendations
- **Progress Visualization**: See your fitness journey with beautiful charts and trends
- **Daily Motivation**: Get inspired with rotating motivational quotes

### 🎯 Habit-Forming Features
- **One-Click Check-ins**: Mark your routine as complete with a simple button click
- **Celebratory Moments**: Enjoy balloons and snow effects when you hit milestones
- **Progress Bars**: Visual feedback showing your streak progress
- **Achievement System**: Earn badges and rewards for staying consistent

### 🌍 International Support
- **Bilingual Interface**: Full Korean and English language support
- **Smart Units**: Automatic unit conversion based on your preferences
- **Cultural Adaptation**: Training styles that match your personality

### 🔐 Privacy & Security
- **Secure Login**: Email-based authentication keeps your data safe
- **Private Data**: Your fitness information stays completely private
- **Easy Recovery**: Simple password reset if you forget your login

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Create Your Account
- Sign up with your email address
- Verify your email (check your inbox)
- Set a strong password

### 2. Complete Your Profile
Tell us about yourself to get personalized recommendations:
- **Basic Info**: Height, weight, gender
- **Fitness Goals**: Fat loss, muscle gain, Special Forces fitness, health maintenance, or mixed
- **Training Style**: Choose your preferred AI coach personality
- **Optional**: Add InBody data for even more precise recommendations

### 3. Start Your Fitness Journey
- **Daily Check-in**: Rate your condition (1-10) and log your mood, energy, and sleep
- **Get Your Routine**: AI generates a personalized workout for the day
- **Complete & Track**: Mark your routine as done and watch your progress

## 📱 How to Use Bootcamper
